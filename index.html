Our Medical Services

Personalised Siddha Healthcare & Patient-Centred Treatment

01 · Consultation

Disease Management & Therapeutic Care

We provide individualised treatment and lifestyle guidance for various health conditions, including:

  • Non-Communicable Diseases (NCDs)Diabetes mellitus, hypertension and other chronic health conditions.
  • Renal CalculiKidney stone management.
  • Gallbladder CalculiGallstone management.
  • PilesHaemorrhoid management.
  • Allergic SinusitisSupportive treatment and symptom management.
  • PCOSPolycystic Ovary Syndrome management.
  • Skin DisordersIndividualised Siddha-based skin care.
  • AnaemiaSupportive care and nutritional guidance.
  • OsteoarthritisJoint health and pain management.
  • Varicose VeinsSymptom management and supportive care.

Our approach emphasises personalised treatment plans, dietary recommendations, lifestyle modifications and continuous health guidance.

02 · Traditional therapies

Siddha External Therapy Services

We offer traditional Siddha external therapies tailored to individual patient needs.

  • Varma TherapyTraditional therapeutic techniques for musculoskeletal and functional concerns.
  • Thokkanam TherapyTherapeutic Siddha massage techniques.
  • Medicated OttradamTherapeutic fomentation using medicated materials.
  • Medicated Kattu TherapyTraditional medicated bandaging applications for selected conditions, including diabetic ulcers and venous ulcers, following appropriate clinical assessment.
